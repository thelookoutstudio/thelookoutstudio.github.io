About the studio

Always looking
up and out.

The Lookout Studio is an independent game studio making memorable experiences for people who like to explore, experiment, and find something unexpected.

We believe every great adventure begins with a new point of view.

Our work starts with a simple spark of curiosity. We follow it into unfamiliar places, shape it into approachable play, and fill the journey with details worth noticing.

Whether a player has a few minutes or a whole evening, we want each visit to our worlds to feel inviting, rewarding, and a little surprising.

Let’s chart a new course.

Have a question, a project idea, or a possible collaboration? Get in touch and tell us what you’re imagining.
